How Scan2Play works

Guests scan a QR code and ask for songs. The AI checks every request: is it a song, does it fit the vibe, has it played tonight already. The queue shows you the ones that passed, and you play them from your own DJ software. The ones the AI turned down are in the history, with the AI's comment, so you can always check that it judges well. Scan2Play plays nothing itself.

Getting started

Log in with Google, or with an e-mail address and a password. Your panel and your party are set up by themselves.

Party and QR code

The vibe, e.g. "Wedding Classics": the AI judges the requests by it. The vibe notes are optional, but the AI reads them with every request and they count for more than the vibe itself. You can use them to add to the vibe ("wedding 40+, no rap") or to replace it: when you play your own mix, pick the vibe "Any" and describe it yourself ("Polish 90s, disco polo and rock, no techno"). Requests that clearly go against them, the AI turns down. Then Who plays (guests see "🎧 Playing: DJ Alex"); they see the vibe and the notes too. The AI's comments can be classic, funny, sarcastic or short. Here you also switch on the guests' dedications.

Print poster and cards

The QR code as an A4 poster or eight table cards. Over the print you can type your own heading and one line of text (e.g. "Anna and Tom's wedding"), pick the language, a theme (classic, elegant, club, pastel) and the heading's colour, and hide what you don't want on paper; "Save" keeps it with the party. You can also copy the party's link and send it to the guests.

📺 Screen for a TV

A page for a TV or a projector: a big QR code and the most wanted songs, refreshed by itself. Open it on a computer connected to the screen.

Settings

The rarer settings are on the Settings tab (beside "DJ Panel" and "History"), in four groups, each folded to one line with its state beside the name:

What is in each group
  • The party: Party hours, Guest limits (2 requests per guest, then a 3-minute wait, by default), The hosts' list (🚫 do not play, ⭐ must play; the people you play for can fill it in themselves through their own link) and Guest kiosk.
  • For the guests: Guest theme, Wishes and party plan, Question for the guests.
  • You as the DJ: Profiles, contact and tips (Instagram, an e-mail and a phone for bookings, a tip link), Your logo (your guests see it instead of Scan2Play's), Your library (at each request you see "✓ In library", "? Similar" or "✗ Not in library", and the button "Hide “Not in library”" hides the ones you don't have) and Morning summary.
  • Access: Staff, This device and Party code (a new QR code when the old one should stop working).

Before the party

A wedding, an 18th, a company party: before you play, you can gather the guests' wishes and agree the evening's plan with the hosts. Both are in Party settings → For the guests → Wishes and party plan (Pro), at an event with a name and a date (e.g. "Anna and Jake's wedding", 14 June).

Print invitations

Wishes before the party: "Create the guests' link" gives the event's link — send it with the invitation or print its QR code on cards for the invitations. Each guest writes up to 5 songs; nobody sees anyone else's. You see them counted, the most wished first, with "✓ In library" from your library, and move the ones you pick to the hosts' "⭐ Must play". Lines without an artist are listed apart, "To check". You collect wishes for up to 5 events at once; a week after the party they are deleted by themselves.

📋 Party plan

Every event has a party plan — the evening’s moments (the couple’s entrance, the first dance, the cake…) with a time, a song and notes, and the event’s “don’t play” and “must play” lists. Start from a template (wedding, 18th, birthday, company party) and send the hosts their link: they fill in the plan themselves, without an account, and you see the changes at once. On the day, “Use the lists at the party” turns the event’s lists on, and “Print the plan” gives you a sheet for the console. That evening “📋 Tonight’s plan” stands above the queue with the next moment and the time to it, and a request for a song of the plan is marked “📋 In the plan”.

During the party

New requests appear in the queue by themselves. A guest asking for a song that already waits counts as a vote. Sort by "Votes" to have the most wanted on top.

▶ Played

When you play the song. It leaves the queue for the history, and the AI won't let it through again. With the program “Played by itself” it happens without a click.

⏭ Skip

When you don't have it or it doesn't fit now. For 2 hours guests can't ask for it again.

Undo

A mistake? After "Played" and "Skip" there's "Undo" at the bottom of the screen for 8 seconds. Later, in the history: ↩ Restore (for played and skipped songs alike).

+ Add a song

Type the song over the queue. It goes into the queue without the AI check, marked 🎧, and the guests can vote for it. The hosts' "do not play" list does not stop it, it only marks it 🚫.

⚠ Check

None of the guest's words are in the song's name, so the AI may have guessed another song. Compare it with the guest's words under the title.

⭐ Hosts’ wish

A song from the "must play" list.

Preview

YouTube's results for a song you don't know. They open in a new tab.

Copy

The artist and the title go to the clipboard: paste them into the search of Serato, VirtualDJ or rekordbox (Ctrl+V).

Keyboard shortcuts on a computer

↑ ↓ (or a click) choose a request, C (or Ctrl+C) copies, Enter is "Played", S is "Skip", Backspace (or Ctrl+Z) is "Undo" (while its bar is shown), Esc chooses none.

💸

Every song has a number, e.g. #27, and guests may write it in a payment's title. When you see the payment in your bank, type the number in "Search" and tap 💸 at the song. Scan2Play never sees any payment.

Clear the queue

Everything that waits goes to the history as cleared.

End Party

Guests can't send requests, and the queue stays. "Resume the party" opens it again, e.g. after a break.

🗳 Question for the guests

The button by the QR code in the panel (or Party settings → For the guests; Pro): ask the room a question of your own, e.g. "Disco polo: yes or no?", with 2–4 answers (left empty they are "Yes" and "No"). Guests see it on their phones above the request field — also while you have paused the requests — and on the TV screen. Each votes once and may change their mind, and you see the results live.

More on questions: results, the TV screen, corrections

"Ask now" or "Save for later", e.g. a few questions ready before the party. One question is asked at a time: a new one ends the one before, and it ends by itself after 12 hours. Guests see the results right after voting or — when you pick "Only when I show them", e.g. for a game — only after "👀 Show the results". "On the TV: Question / Songs" picks what the screen shows. A question saved for later is changed by "Edit"; one already asked by "Correct as a new one" (the old one stays with its votes).

⏰ Party hours

Party settings → Party hours: the party opens and closes by itself at the times you pick (Polish time), e.g. "today 20:00" and "tomorrow 3:00". The panel shows it under the heading, ✕ cancels. When you end or resume the party by hand, your choice wins and the next planned time stays. Before the opening, guests see "Requests from 20:00".

🖥 Guest kiosk

Party settings → Guest kiosk (Pro): a link for a tablet at the bar. Guests without a phone type their requests and vote there, with the most wanted beside them. "Next guest" starts afresh for the next person; the tablet does it by itself after a minute without a touch (20 seconds after the AI's answer).

Setting up the tablet and the kiosk's limits

Setting up the tablet: "Make the kiosk link", copy it and open it on the tablet in a browser where you are not logged in — logged in, the kiosk shows a warning, because a guest who leaves it would find your panel. So that nobody leaves the kiosk, turn on Guided Access on an iPad (Settings → Accessibility), screen pinning on Android. The address bar shows just "/k", without the link's secret.

The kiosk has its own limits, since every guest types on one tablet: 3 requests a minute and 20 👍 per 10 minutes. Your limit per guest counts for each person on their own. "New link" turns the old tablet off (e.g. when someone learned the link), "Turn off" takes the link away. Requests from the kiosk carry "🖥 kiosk" in the queue and the history, and the evening summary counts them as "From the kiosk" (in the CSV too).

Played, from your DJ software

No need to click “Played”: a small program on the computer you play from reads VirtualDJ’s or Serato’s history (or what Spotify and TIDAL play) in the background and marks the queue’s songs played by itself. The panel need not be open. The Pro plan, Windows or Mac.

🎧 From DJ software

The button above the queue on a computer (beside “+ Add a song”) opens a card with three steps.

How to connect
  1. Download the program: Windows, Mac M1 and later or Mac Intel.
  2. Unpack the zip and start “Scan2Play Zagrane”. The first time Windows or macOS asks whether to run it — the card says what to click.
  3. “Connect a computer” in the card: type the 6-digit code (valid 10 minutes) into the program’s window and click “Połącz”.
At the party

Start the program before the first song and play as usual. When you play a song a guest asked for, the request goes to the history as played by itself: from Serato within a few seconds, from VirtualDJ about 45 seconds after it starts (VirtualDJ waits that long before writing it to its history), from Spotify and TIDAL after 30 seconds of playing. A song only loaded on a deck does not count.

It reads VirtualDJ, Serato DJ (Pro and Lite) and the Spotify and TIDAL apps on the computer (TIDAL on Windows only; on a Mac, TIDAL inside VirtualDJ or Serato goes to their history). With rekordbox, Traktor and djay you click “Played” as before.

Connection, the program's window and privacy
Once for good

It stays connected: at the next party just start the program (also after a new party code). You can connect up to 3 computers, e.g. a laptop and a spare. “Disconnect” in the card or “Odłącz ten komputer” in the program ends it.

When it marks

When the song in your DJ software has the request’s artist and title (as “✓ Have” in your library) and started after the request. A file without tags, a title written differently? Click “Played” yourself — the button works as always.

The program’s window

It says whether it works, which history it reads (“Czyta”) and which of the queue’s songs it marked (“Oznaczone”). Closed, it hides by the clock (on a Mac in the menu bar) and keeps working. It does not see the history, because VirtualDJ or Serato keeps it in an unusual place (e.g. an external drive)? “Wskaż folder historii…” (pick the history folder; when it reads something already: “VirtualDJ albo Serato nie na liście? Wskaż ich folder…”) and choose that program’s folder: “VirtualDJ” or “_Serato_” (Serato 4/5: “Serato\Library”, on a Mac “Serato/Library”). One folder at a time; the usual places are read anyway.

Spotify and TIDAL

On Windows Spotify or TIDAL just has to play: the program asks Windows what it plays (what Windows shows by its own media controls). On a Mac (Spotify only), the first time, macOS asks whether “Scan2Play Zagrane” may control Spotify — allow it: the program only reads what plays (“brak zgody macOS”, no consent, in the program’s window: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation). No Spotify or TIDAL login.

Privacy

The program only reads the history (and, from Spotify and TIDAL, what plays now) and changes nothing in VirtualDJ, Serato, Spotify or TIDAL. The files stay on the computer: only the artist, title and time of the songs reach Scan2Play, and none of it is kept.

On a phone

Each request is a card with its title, votes, ⚠ / ⭐ and the "Played" / "Skip" buttons. Tap the card (or ▾) to see the guest's words, the AI's comment, "Preview" and 💸.

Notifications of new requests: Party settings → This device. On an iPhone add the panel to the Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen); without it the iPhone won't show them.

Install the app in the same place: the panel then opens like an app, with its icon on the screen.

After the party

The history shows everything that played, was skipped, turned down by the AI (with its comment, why) or cleared, day by day, for 30 days.

Evening summary

How many requests, what played, what guests wanted but didn't hear, the most requested artists. To print, as a PDF or a CSV.

Morning summary: the morning after an evening with at least 5 guests' requests, at 10:00 Polish time, you get the evening's numbers and its top 3 by e-mail. You switch it on in Party settings → You as the DJ (Pro).

Clear the history

Deletes the played and rejected requests with the guests' words. The AI then no longer knows what has played.

The party's staff

Other people can help you at the party, each with their own account. Go to Party settings → Staff and type the address of the person's account, or send them a link. For each you pick a role: what they may do. The staff is at most 10 people.

View only

Sees the queue and the history, changes nothing.

Queue

Played, Skip and Undo, adding songs, tips 💸, clearing the queue, ending and resuming the party.

Co-organiser

The same, and the party's settings too: the vibe, the hosts' list, the guest limits, the guest kiosk, the wishes and party plan, and the questions for the guests.

Custom

You tick the permissions one by one.

Nobody but you can change your profiles, tip link, staff or account.

What guests see

They scan the code and type the artist and the title. In a moment they see the answer: YES! or NO, with the AI's comment. When the AI does not answer for a while, the request reaches you unchecked and the guest sees "SENT".

They see the list of waiting requests and can give 👍 to someone else's song. The limits keep you from being flooded with requests.

When you switch on dedications, a guest can add one to a request, "💌 Add a dedication" (up to 100 characters, e.g. "For Anna on her 18th"). Only you see it, under the song in the queue.

When you ask a question, they see it above the request field, a button for each answer. Once they vote it folds to one line ("🗳 … · Yours: No"), the results inside — or "The DJ will show the results." and "↻ Check the results" while you keep them hidden.

The guest theme (Party settings → For the guests) changes the look of the guest page, the kiosk and the TV screen: Classic, Wedding, Christmas, Confetti or Scary for Halloween.

When you give an e-mail or a phone for bookings (Profiles, contact and tips), guests see "Book this DJ" under an accepted request and after the party, and write or call you directly. Scan2Play never sees that message.

When you upload your logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB), your guests see it at the top of the party page, and Scan2Play steps down to a small "Powered by Scan2Play" at the bottom. The logo is on the poster, the cards, the TV screen and the evening summary too. We trim the picture's empty margins ourselves.
