How Scan2Play works

Guests scan a QR code and ask for songs. The AI checks every request: is it a song, does it fit the vibe, has it played tonight already. The queue shows you the ones that passed, and you play them from your own DJ software. The ones the AI turned down are in the history, with the AI's comment, so you can always check that it judges well. Scan2Play plays nothing itself.

Getting started

Log in with Google. Your panel and your party are set up by themselves.

Settings

The vibe, e.g. "Wedding Classics": the AI judges the requests by it. Optionally a vibe notes ("wedding 40+, no rap") and Who plays (guests see "🎧 Playing: DJ Adam"). The AI's comments can be classic, funny, sarcastic or short.

Print poster and cards

The QR code as an A4 poster or eight table cards. You can also copy the party's link and send it to the guests.

More ›

The rarer settings: The hosts' list (🚫 do not play, ⭐ must play; the people you play for can fill it in themselves through their own link), Profiles and tips (Instagram, a tip link), Guest limits (2 requests per guest, then a 3-minute wait, by default), Staff and This device.

During the party

New requests appear in the queue by themselves. A guest asking for a song that already waits counts as a vote. Sort by "Votes" to have the most wanted on top.

▶ Played

When you play the song. It leaves the queue for the history, and the AI won't let it through again.

⏭ Skip

When you don't have it or it doesn't fit now. For 2 hours guests can't ask for it again.

Undo

A mistake? After "Played" and "Skip" there's "Undo" at the bottom of the screen for 8 seconds. Later, in the history: ↩ Restore (for played and skipped songs alike).

⚠ Check

None of the guest's words are in the song's name, so the AI may have guessed another song. Compare it with the guest's words under the title.

⭐ Hosts’ wish

A song from the "must play" list.

Preview

YouTube's results for a song you don't know. They open in a new tab.

Copy

The artist and the title go to the clipboard: paste them into the search of Serato, VirtualDJ or rekordbox (Ctrl+V). On a computer the keyboard works too: ↑ ↓ (or a click) choose a request, C copies, Enter is "Played", S is "Skip", Backspace is "Undo" (while its bar is shown), Esc chooses none.

💸

Every song has a number, e.g. #27, and guests may write it in a payment's title. When you see the payment in your bank, type the number in "Search" and tap 💸 at the song. Scan2Play never sees any payment.

Clear the queue

Everything that waits goes to the history as cleared.

End Party

Guests can't send requests, and the queue stays. "Resume the party" opens it again, e.g. after a break.

On a phone

Each request is a card with its title, votes, ⚠ / ⭐ and the "Played" / "Skip" buttons. Tap the card (or ▾) to see the guest's words, the AI's comment, "Preview" and 💸.

Notifications of new requests: Party settings → This device. On an iPhone add the panel to the Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen); without it the iPhone won't show them.

Install the app in the same place: the panel then opens like an app, with its icon on the screen.

After the party

The history shows everything that played, was skipped, turned down by the AI (with its comment, why) or cleared, day by day, for 30 days.

Evening summary

How many requests, what played, what guests wanted but didn't hear, the most requested artists. To print, as a PDF or a CSV.

Clear the history

Deletes the played and rejected requests with the guests' words. The AI then no longer knows what has played.

The party's staff

Other people can help you at the party, each with their own Google account. Go to Party settings → Staff and type the address of the person's account, or send them a link. For each you pick a role: what they may do. The staff is at most 10 people.

View only

Sees the queue and the history, changes nothing.

Queue

Played, Skip and Undo, tips 💸, clearing the queue, ending and resuming the party.

Co-organiser

The same, and the party's settings too: the vibe, the hosts' list and the guest limits.

Custom

You tick the permissions one by one.

Nobody but you can change your profiles, tip link, staff or account.

What guests see

They scan the code and type a title, an artist or a line of the lyrics. In a moment they see the answer: YES! or NO, with the AI's comment. When the AI does not answer for a while, the request reaches you unchecked and the guest sees "SENT".

They see the list of waiting requests and can give 👍 to someone else's song. The limits keep you from being flooded with requests.
