Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Scan2Play ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered music request platform for events. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our service at scan2play.com.pl.

2. Data We Collect

For DJs (registered users):

  • Google account profile information (name, email, profile picture) via OAuth 2.0
  • Party settings and configuration preferences
  • If the DJ gives them: the addresses of the DJ’s Instagram, Facebook and TikTok profiles — shown publicly to the guests on the party page and on the QR print
  • If the DJ gives it: the link to the DJ’s tip page (Revolut, PayPal, buycoffee.to, Suppi, Tipply, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi) — shown publicly to the guests on the party page, under an accepted request and on the QR print
  • Feedback messages submitted through the dashboard
  • If the DJ switches on notifications on a device: that browser’s push subscription (the push service’s address of the browser and its encryption keys) and the dashboard’s language
  • If someone joins a party’s staff (a bartender, a second DJ) by the owner’s invitation link: their Google account’s identifier, the name on that account and what the owner allows them to do — the party’s owner sees the name on the list of the staff
  • The party owner’s name from their Google account: their party’s staff see it, and so does whoever opens their invitation link (when the party has no “Who plays”)

For Guests (anonymous users):

  • Song request text (a title, an artist or a line of the lyrics) — kept as typed (up to 150 characters) and shown to the party's DJ beside the song the AI picked; please do not type personal data into it
  • A session identifier (a cookie) — for the request limit and to recognise your earlier requests and your 👍 at the party (marked “Yours”, a vote counted once); no personal data
  • IP address — in the server's memory only, for the limits of requests and of 👍 from one network at a party (against abuse); never stored in the database or in the application's logs

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate DJs and manage party sessions
  • To process song requests and provide AI-powered music evaluation
  • To count requests for the same song and the guests’ 👍 as votes and show guests the most wanted songs (the number of votes holds no personal data)
  • To enforce rate limits and prevent abuse

4. Links to Other Sites

Scan2Play plays no music and uses no YouTube API. The DJ’s “🔍 Preview” button only opens YouTube’s search results for a song in the DJ’s own browser — what YouTube does there is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The Instagram, Facebook and TikTok buttons on the party page lead to the DJ’s profiles there. Scan2Play embeds none of their content or scripts — the site opens only when a guest clicks, and its own privacy rules apply there.

The “💸 Tip the DJ” button leads to the DJ’s page on a tipping service. The payment happens only there, straight to the DJ: Scan2Play takes no part in it, sees neither the amount nor any payment details and takes no commission. The tip and its settlement are between the guest, the DJ and that service, on its terms.

5. Data Retention

  • Song requests: automatically deleted 30 days after they were made (or earlier, when the DJ deletes their account)
  • Sessions (the DJ’s login, a guest’s session identifier): stored in the database and deleted after 30 minutes without activity
  • A guest’s IP address: in the server’s memory only, forgotten 10 minutes after the last request or 👍 from that network
  • DJ account data: stored until the DJ deletes their account
  • A device’s notification subscription: until the DJ switches the notifications off on it, the browser drops it, or the DJ deletes their account
  • A place on a party’s staff: until the owner takes the access away, or the staff member or the owner deletes their account

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only sent to Google (OAuth login) and Google Gemini (AI song evaluation — it receives the guest’s request text) as required for the service to function. While a guest types, their browser asks Apple’s iTunes Search API for song suggestions (it receives the typed text, nothing else). When a DJ has switched on notifications on a device, a new request is sent to it as an end-to-end encrypted message (the song’s name) through the push service of the DJ’s browser (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft), which delivers it but cannot read it.

7. Your Rights — Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by clicking "Delete Account" in your DJ Dashboard. This permanently removes your party settings, song request history, and feedback. You may also revoke Scan2Play's access from your Google Account settings.
