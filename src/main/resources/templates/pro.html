Scan2Play Pro

Everything a party needs is free. Pro is for DJs who play often: lists from the hosts, your profiles and tips, the evening summary.

Tell us you want Pro

There is no payment in the app yet — we switch Pro on after your message to scan2playcontact@gmail.com.

Free

Free, always

  • The QR code, a poster and table cards
  • Requests checked by the AI: is it a song, does it fit the vibe, has it played tonight
  • The queue: Played, Skip, Undo, Copy, keys
  • The guests' votes and 👍, the vibe, its note and "Who's playing"
  • History of 30 days, notifications on your phone
  • Staff: 1 person
  • Guest limits: 2 requests per guest, then a 3 minute break, no repeats of the last 15

Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

  • The hosts’ list🚫 what not to play, ⭐ what to play for sure — the hosts fill it in themselves by a link
  • Your profiles and tipsInstagram, Facebook, TikTok and "Tip the DJ" for the guests and on the print, the 💸 count in the queue
  • The evening summaryto print, as a PDF and a CSV
  • AI comments with characterfunny, sarcastic or short
  • Your own guest limitshow many requests per guest, how long a break, how many songs without repeats
  • Your library✓ or ✗ at each request: whether you have the song — from your rekordbox collection or an M3U playlist
  • Staff of up to 10a second DJ, a bartender, the organiser — each with the permissions you give them

Good to know

What if Pro ends?

Nothing is lost. The lists, the profiles and the settings wait and come back with Pro; the staff keep their access.

Do the staff need Pro?

No. The staff work on the plan of the party's organiser.

And the guests?

The guests pay nothing and install nothing: they scan the code and ask for a song.
