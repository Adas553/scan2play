2. Description of Service

Scan2Play is an AI-powered music request platform for events. DJs create party sessions, guests request songs via QR code, and AI evaluates whether each request fits the party vibe; requests for the same song are counted as votes. Music is played through YouTube or Spotify, or — in the “Your DJ software” mode — by the DJ’s own software, and Scan2Play only collects and filters the requests.
